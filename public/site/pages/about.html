Our Story

iDelta was founded in 2023 by a team of educators and technologists in Navi Mumbai who saw a gap — students struggling to find quality tutors nearby, and talented teachers unable to reach students who needed them most.

We built iDelta to bridge that gap: a platform where finding a trusted home tutor or solving a doubt live with an expert is as simple as a few taps.

Today, iDelta is Navi Mumbai's most trusted educational marketplace, serving students from Class 1 through college across all boards and subjects.
